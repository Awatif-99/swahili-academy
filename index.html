You don't need to know a single word to begin. Explore four useful expressions, then try a quick practice question.

SWAHILI ESSENTIALS01 / Greetings
JamboHello
Habari?How are you?
AsanteThank you
KaribuWelcome

A little context: Habari? is a common way to ask how someone is. Try replying Nzuri, asante — “Good, thank you.”
